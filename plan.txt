O. DUPLICATE PREVENTION
=======================================================================

Potential duplicate pathways include:

- Apple Watch saves to HealthKit, then watch sync reports completion
- iPhone active workout saves to HealthKit, then HealthKit import sees it
- User manually completes a workout, then HealthKit imports a matching workout
- WorkoutKit schedule completion appears through HealthKit
- App relaunch repeats an anchored query result
- Temporary watch disconnection retries a transfer
- User taps save multiple times
- App Intent and in-app action occur close together

Use stable provider identifiers and idempotency keys.

Write explicit tests for every duplicate pathway.

The app must never display two completed workouts for one execution merely
because it was observed through two Apple frameworks.
